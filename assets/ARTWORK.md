# Artwork

Original conceptual illustrations generated for Ludwig using the built-in image generation tool. These are artistic interpretations, not archival photographs or evidence about Wittgenstein's biography. Both final assets are optimized WebP images, 1320 × 880 pixels.

## portrait.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape hero illustration for Ludwig, a welcoming offline philosophy reading app. Primary request: a recognizable illustrated portrait of philosopher Ludwig Wittgenstein, thoughtful, angular face and swept dark hair, seated by a simple window with a Norwegian fjord, pine-covered mountains and still water beyond. Editorial Viennese modernist woodcut / hand-pulled screenprint illustration, sophisticated flat shapes and delicate imperfect ink texture, restrained natural geometry, warm ivory paper, deep pine green, pale sage, muted terracotta and ochre. Landscape composition 3:2, head and upper torso at left-center looking toward the fjord on the right, generous airy landscape; dignified, reflective, curious, quietly inviting, distinctive thoughtful portrait, no caricature. Clearly artistic interpretation rather than historical photograph. No words, letters, signatures, border or watermark.

## practices.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: conceptual landscape illustration for Ludwig philosophy app, illustrating language as shared ordinary practices. Primary request: an inviting small European village square and workshop, a carpenter and neighbor coordinating a simple task, a person at a market counter, a child and adult with a board game on a table by an open window. Compose these as a single coherent layered scene, showing ordinary activities and human cooperation, with mountains and pines far behind. Sophisticated Viennese modernist woodcut / hand-pulled screenprint, flat geometric forms with hand-cut ink texture, warm ivory, deep pine green, pale sage, subdued terracotta and ochre; same quiet editorial art direction as a philosophical reading journal. Wide landscape 3:2, intelligible and uncluttered with small faceless figures and beautifully simple objects, warmth and breathing space. Conceptual illustration rather than historical reconstruction or literal argument diagram. No text, letters, numbers, speech bubbles, border, signature or watermark.
